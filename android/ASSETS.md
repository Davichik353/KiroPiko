# Android assets for KiroPiko

The two PNG atlases in `app/src/main/assets/kiro/` are exact copies of the approved KiroPiko Windows assets in `windows/public/nova/`: `kiro-mint-states.png` and `kiro-holidays.png`. Generation provenance and prompts are recorded in `windows/public/nova/ASSETS.md`.

The app icon is copied from `windows/src-tauri/icons/icon.png`. Android status-bar and widget preview marks use a simple K glyph. Runtime widgets use the Kiro painter.

The 14 WAV files are exact copies from `windows/public/sounds/`, generated by `windows/scripts/gen-nova-sounds.mjs`. They replace the upstream sounds.

Original MIT source attribution and library licences remain in app assets and the source repository. Upstream namespaces and pairing protocol are retained for compatibility, not presented as the product name.
